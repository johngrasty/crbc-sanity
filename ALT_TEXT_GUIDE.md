# AI-Powered Alt Text Guide for Content Editors

## What Changed?

We've enabled **Sanity AI Assist** to automatically generate alt text for images! This helps make our website more accessible for people using screen readers.

## ⚠️ First-Time Setup (Admin Only)

**Before anyone can use AI Assist, an admin must enable it once:**

1. Open Sanity Studio
2. Open any document
3. Click the **sparkle (✨) icon** in the toolbar
4. Click **"Enable Sanity AI Assist"** button
5. This creates an API token automatically

This only needs to be done once by someone with admin/developer permissions. After that, everyone can use AI Assist!

## For New Images

When you upload a new image, AI Assist will **automatically generate alt text** for you. You can:
- ✅ Accept the AI-generated text if it's accurate
- ✏️ Edit it to add more context or fix any issues
- 🔄 Regenerate it if you don't like the first version

## Writing Good Alt Text

Whether using AI or writing manually, good alt text should:

✅ **Be descriptive**: "Portrait of John Smith smiling in a blue shirt"  
❌ **Not generic**: "Person" or "Image"

✅ **Be concise**: Under 125 characters when possible  
❌ **Not too long**: Avoid full paragraphs

✅ **Include context**: "Youth group volunteers serving at food bank"  
❌ **Not redundant**: Don't start with "Image of..." or "Picture of..."

✅ **Describe what matters**: Focus on what's important about the image  
❌ **Not overly technical**: Avoid unnecessary details

## Examples

### Staff Photos
- ✅ Good: "Professional portrait of Karen Holcomb"
- ✅ Good: "Headshot of Pastor John Grasty in formal attire"
- ❌ Bad: "Person"
- ❌ Bad: "Image of a staff member"

### Announcement Images
- ✅ Good: "Candlelit Christmas Eve service with decorated sanctuary"
- ✅ Good: "Children participating in Easter egg hunt on church lawn"
- ❌ Bad: "Church event"
- ❌ Bad: "Announcement image"

### Ministry Images
- ✅ Good: "Youth group students studying Bible together"
- ✅ Good: "Volunteers preparing meals in church kitchen"
- ❌ Bad: "Ministry activity"
- ❌ Bad: "Group of people"

## Why This Matters

- **Accessibility**: Screen readers read alt text aloud to visually impaired users
- **SEO**: Search engines use alt text to understand images
- **Compliance**: Required by WCAG accessibility standards
- **User Experience**: Alt text shows when images fail to load
